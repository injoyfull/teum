틈.

A teum to pause, a teum to rest.
In that gap, a story — sent as a gift.

Some days you finish your coffee and can't recall the taste. Some days your eyes are open from morning to night, and you haven't really seen a thing. Even on those days, there is a gap between breathing and resting. Small, but it is there. Teum (틈) is the Korean word for that gap — and this is a letter sent in the hope that you will enjoy yours. Once every two weeks: one sense, and one pebble. Small enough to drop into your day and leave a ripple. Nothing heavier than that. It comes the way you would ask after a friend — how have you been?

We collect only your email address, and only to send you this letter. It is never used for anything else and never shared. If you unsubscribe, or if the letter ends, it is deleted right away.

Thank you. Your first teum is on its way.

How Teum works

01

One sense, every two weeks

Color, texture, sound, taste, smell — and the ones in between that have no name. One sense at a time. Not about the season, but about what you can feel today.

02

One pebble

If it's grand, you won't do it. The first bite with your eyes closed. Ten steps without earphones. Small things — dropped in gently, leaving a ripple.

03

A reader's sense

Reply to any letter, and the moment you woke up can appear in the next one, anonymously. Not a letter you read alone, but one we wake up in together.
