Have you stood where
shade meets sun?

Season 1 · Summer Sense Diving No.05

Season 1 Summer Sense Diving · This teum — Temperature

I was avoiding the heat well,
and missing the summer entirely.

It was at a crosswalk. Nobody had arranged it, and yet people were standing in a line in the shade of a utility pole. Six or seven of them, in one narrow strip of shade. I slipped in too.

It was one step. From sun into shade. And in that one step the season changed. The thermometer would have shown the same number, I'm sure. It goes cool at the back of your neck first. Your shoulders come down.

Summer was only on the calendar

Come to think of it, I spend the whole day in one temperature myself. Home, the subway, the office, the café. It's summer, and there's little reason to sweat, and little reason to be grateful for shade.

Avoiding the heat, have I ended up avoiding the summer along with it? That's what I found myself thinking.

Shade is made by sun

Under the shade I thought something else too. Where did this coolness come from? If the sun weren't hot, would this shade have tasted so sweet?

Some of you may be walking through the middle of the sun right now. Shade isn't far. One tree is enough. One utility pole will do. And there is room in the shade. Room where people end up standing side by side, without ever arranging it.

— oLiHANNA

This teum's pebble · Temperature

Cross the line between shade and sun, slowly

If you meet the edge of a shadow while walking today, stop there for a moment. Half a step in the sun, half a step in the shade. Standing on the line, wearing both seasons at once. Then cross over very slowly. That one step will be longer than you'd think.

What temperature did you meet today? Just reply to this letter. The line you crossed could appear in the next one, under ‘A reader's sense’, anonymously.

The whole temperature of summer is right there.
